# Lookout hunt (`HuntShop`, `HuntLookout`)

`src/scenes/minigames/hunt/`: a lookout tower (`Spot.kind: 'tower'`, drawn by `addTower` in `city/draw.ts`) stands in the forest clearing, north of the town; its door is at the foot of the ladder and is a `wide` door (`inDoorZone()` in `town.ts`: it opens from the sides too, not only from below). The stage `lookout` always opens `HuntShop` first (`enterStage()`), which goes on to `HuntLookout`.

In `HuntLookout` you shoot from the tower in first person (your rifle at the bottom of the screen like the forest, no tower or hunter in view; tap; hold to keep firing with an `auto` rifle) at 60 deer wandering over a one-screen meadow; every deer stays where it fell (all 60 can be fetched, the win needs 10), and you must climb down (the button only shows after the first `CLIMB_DOWN_AT` = 5 deer are shot), walk to one with the floating `Joystick` (free walking; the view stays first person: sideways the camera follows you across scenery that reaches `PAD` px either side, forwards pushes everything on the meadow down the screen and makes it bigger by hand (`oy`, `sy()`, `updateView()`: no camera scroll in y), things behind you are hidden, the lookout stands in the field, your rifle hangs low and arrows point at off-screen deer; so HUD objects need `setScrollFactor(0)`), carry it back to the ladder's foot (one at a time, slower) and climb up again.

A deer has `DEER_HP` (2): a rifle with `dmg` 1 needs two hits (the first wounds it: it stays reddish, bleeds, bolts and then limps), the double-barrel one. When the hunter climbs down all the other deer run out of the meadow and are gone (`leaveDir`, `hasLeft`); no new ones come while you are on the ground, and the herd walks in again once you are back up (new deer come out of the forest: from behind the far trees or in over the left/right edge, `Deer.entering`, never popping up on the grass), so a carcass can be fetched in peace. 60 shot and 10 carried is a win (`win(starsFor(misses, [20, 50]))`; coins are earned per deer shot and per deer delivered, not by `pay: true`).

The rules are Phaser-free in `huntLogic.ts` (`onKill`, `isWon`, `Magazine` for ammo/cooldown/reload, `pickTarget`); the rifles are in `huntWeapons.ts`. A rifle costs coins (`pay()`) and also needs a total of stars (`totalStars() >= w.stars`, `unlocked()`); stars are only a threshold and are never spent. Owned rifles and the one in hand are in `huntSave` (`core/huntSave.ts`, `hunt-save`, own file apart from `forestSave`).

## The hound

The pet shop also sells the hunting dog (`'hound'` in `Pet`, 100 coins; art `hound` for the shop and the town, `hound-run-1/2` for the hunt): black, bigger, with a green jacket. Dogs and the hound are sold in any of 10 coats (`PET_COLORS` in `core/profile.ts`: brown, black, white and the rainbow); the shop shows paint pots under them, `profile.color` keeps the choice, and every animal and every coat is bought on its own (`profile.owned`, keyed by the texture name; `hasPet`/`takePet`/`isCurrentPet`), but one you own is taken again for free (♥ under the cage = owned, ✔ = the one following you) and `petTexture()`/`houndRunTexture()` name the art (`dog-<color>`, `hound-<color>`, `hound-run-<1|2>-<color>`: 10 files each, so a new coat means new SVGs, and the profile test fails until they exist). With it as your pet, the dog fetches the deer you shoot, one at a time, from the ladder's foot to the carcass and back (`Dog`/`updateDog` in `HuntLookout`, `onDogPickUp`/`onDogDeliver`, `stepToward` in `huntLogic.ts`), so you never have to climb down.

Dev only: `?hound` gives you the dog for free, `?deer=N` starts with N deer already shot. The art is rasterised at 1.5x (`Boot`), so a sprite's display size is its SVG size times 1.5 times the scale.
